Build a Python 3.12 application for a small business that manages customers, products, orders and invoices. Customers can place orders containing multiple products. Products belong to categories and have stock quantities. Orders can be pending, confirmed, shipped or cancelled. Confirming an order reserves stock. Cancelling a confirmed order releases the reserved stock. An invoice is created when an order is confirmed. Customers should be able to search their orders and invoices. Administrators should be able to manage products and categories. The application must persist all data in SQLite, expose the main operations through a CLI, validate input, handle errors cleanly, keep business logic separate from persistence and remain reasonably modular.
